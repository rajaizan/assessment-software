## [?ctors] Affecting Transpiration in Plants
Rate of transpiration is affected by the following [?vironmental] factors.
- [?] Humidity: Water vapours present in the air affect the rate of transpiration. The rate of transpiration is inversely proportional to the humidity. Rate of transpiration decreases with increase of humidity in the air.
- [?] Temperature: Increase in temperature decreases the humidity and opens the stomata. As a result, rate of transpiration increases.
- [?] Light Intensity: Light increases the rate of transpiration by opening the stomata.
- [?] Air/Wind speed: Rate of transpiration increases with wind speed because the saturated air around the leaves is removed.
