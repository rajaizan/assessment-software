## 1.5 Food making in plants – Photosynthesis
As you know, plants prepare their food themselves. Food making is a multi-step process. It takes place in leaves mainly, where chloroplast is present. The chloroplast contains a green pigment called chlorophyll. It traps heat and light from the sun rays falling over it. Carbon dioxide (air) enters into the leaf cells through stomata and the xylem cells provide water.

## Adaptations of Leaves for Photosynthesis
Leaves are the food factories. Their structure is adapted [f?] photosynthesis, such as:
- i. The broad shape of leaves provide large surface area that receives maximum sunlight.
- ii. Due to the thin leaf-width, carbon dioxide quickly diffuses into the mesophyll cells.
- iii. Inside the leaf, chlorophyll is arranged in thylakoid stacks for receiving maximum energy from sunlight.
