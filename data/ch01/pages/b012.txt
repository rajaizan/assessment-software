## 1.4.2 Translocation (Movement of food)
The food prepared by leaves is transported through [?hloem]. This process is known as translocation. Let us study [?e] structure of phloem.
## [?tructure] of Phloem Vessels
Phloem tissues consists of two main types of cells.
- [?] The sieve tubes: They conduct food and have perforations to filter impurities and regulate the smooth flow of the food.
- [?] Companion cells: They are attached to sieve tubes. They are parenchyma in nature. They receive food from the leaves and store them until needed.
- [?i.] Supportive cells: These cells are sclerenchyma in nature. They are known as fibers and sclereids.
