## iii. Leaves
Leaves are the food factories. Their structures facilitate them in making food.
## Internal structure of a leaf
The internal structure of a leaf has three distinct layers of cells such as epidermis, mesophyll, and vascular bundles. The epidermis is the uppermost and the lowermost layer of a leaf. It is protected by a waxy layer called the cuticle. The lower epidermis contains stomata (special openings for gaseous exchange) surrounded by two guard cells.
