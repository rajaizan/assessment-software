## 1.3 Organ Systems in Plants
A plant has two major organ systems: root system and shoot system.
The root system anchors plants into the soil; absorbs nutrients and water from the soil. Moreover, it provides a shelter to nitrogen-fixing bacteria to provide nitrogen to plants.
