## Key Points
- The root system includes those parts of the plant that present below the ground in soil.
- Shoot system includes those parts of the plants that are present above the ground.
- Xylem cells are known as water conducting cells. Phloem cells transport food produced by the leaves throughout the plant (translocation).
- Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize nutrients from carbon dioxide and water.
- Respiration is a process in living organisms involving the production of energy, typically with the intake of oxygen and release of carbon dioxide from the oxidation of complex organic substances.
- Different factors like temperature, humidity, wind speed and light intensity affect the rate of transpiration.
- Aerobic respiration requires oxygen while anaerobic respiration does not require oxygen.
- Magnesium is used by chlorophyll to capture energy and nitrates are used by plants to produce protein.
